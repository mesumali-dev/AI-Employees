# WhatsApp Outreach Style Guide — Cortech Solution

This guide defines the structure, tone, personalization rubric, and formatting standards for high-converting B2B WhatsApp cold outreach. All drafts generated by the `whatsapp-message-drafter` skill MUST adhere to these rules.

---

## 1. Core Principles of WhatsApp Cold Outreach

WhatsApp is a personal, instant messaging channel — not email. Messages must feel like a thoughtful, peer-to-peer note from a professional engineer, NOT an automated marketing blast.

1. **No Email Formats:** Never include "Subject:", email headers, or formal letter openers ("Dear Sir/Madam").
2. **Mobile First:** Keep paragraphs short (1–2 sentences each) with clean line breaks so the message is readable on a 6-inch phone screen without overwhelming the recipient.
3. **1 Specific Pain Point:** Never list multiple issues. Isolate the single most visible technical defect or commercial opportunity discovered during the website audit (e.g., HTTP/No SSL warning, missing booking flow, slow load speed, outdated template, missing analytics).
4. **Concrete "We Do [X] So You Get [Benefit Y]":** Clearly articulate the technical capability and the commercial outcome in plain business terms.
5. **Zero-Pressure CTA:** Never ask for "15 minutes of your time", a Zoom call, or a phone conversation. Ask for permission to share a quick preview, breakdown, or mockup. Low friction dramatically increases response rates.
6. **Mandatory Sender Identity:** Always conclude with the exact Cortech Solution signature.

## 2. Standard Message Template & Structure

Each message follows this 5-block structure:

```text
[Greeting]

[Context & Genuine Compliment / Observation]

[The Specific Technical Friction or Gap]

[Value Proposition: What We Build & Commercial Benefit]

[Frictionless Call to Action]

Best regards,

Syed Mesum Ali Shah
Cortech Solution
mesum@cortechsolution.com
```

### Block 1: Greeting
- If `Decision Maker` is known: `Hi [First Name],` (e.g. `Hi Maninder,`)
- If only company name is known: `Hi [Company Name] team,` (e.g. `Hi Seasons Consultancy team,`)

### Block 2: Context / Observation
Anchor the message in real context. Mention their location, reputation, or rating:
- *"I came across [Company Name] while looking at engineering firms in Calgary — your 5.0 rating really stands out."*
- *"I was reviewing luxury real estate brokerages in Dubai and came across your portfolio."*

### Block 3: The 1 Pain Point (Observed Friction)
Cite the single most critical problem from `Problems Found` or the lowest audit score:
- **No SSL / HTTP:** *"I noticed [domain] is still served over HTTP with no SSL — browsers now flag that as 'Not Secure', which quietly turns away clients before they ever reach out."*
- **Slow Speed / High Bounce:** *"I ran a quick performance check and noticed the mobile site takes over 5.5s to load, which usually costs 30-40% of prospective clients on mobile."*
- **Generic Intake Form:** *"I noticed the site relies on a standard contact form with a generic info@ email and no direct booking flow."*
- **Missing AI / After-Hours Capture:** *"I noticed you receive high inquiry volumes during weekends and evenings when your front desk is offline."*

### Block 4: Value Proposition
- **Engineering / Healthcare / Real Estate:** *"We build fast, custom intake and booking systems for [Industry], so every high-value inquiry is automatically captured and organized without slipping through the cracks."*
- **Security / Web Redesign:** *"We handle fast security upgrades and modern web infrastructure for [Industry], so your visitors immediately see a verified, trusted site instead of a browser warning."*
- **AI Chatbot:** *"We build 24/7 AI intake assistants trained on your portfolio/services, so prospective clients get instant answers and booked consultations round the clock."*

### Block 5: The CTA
Keep it conversational and low-commitment:
- *"Let me know if you'd like me to send over a quick preview of how this works."*
- *"Would you be open to seeing a 2-minute preview of what this looks like for [Company Name]?"*

### Block 6: Mandatory Signature
Do not alter, omit, or replace this sign-off:
```text
Best regards,

Syed Mesum Ali Shah
Cortech Solution
mesum@cortechsolution.com
```

## 3. Phone Number Sanitization & Formatting

WhatsApp Web requires phone numbers formatted in strictly numeric international E.164 format (country code + subscriber number, without `+`, spaces, dashes, or leading zeros).

| Country | CSV Raw Input Example | Normalized WhatsApp Number | wa.me Link Format |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **United Arab Emirates (UAE)** | `+971 58 103 7571` | `971581037571` | `https://wa.me/971581037571?text=...` |
| **United States / Canada** | `+1 (613) 795-8331` | `16137958331` | `https://wa.me/16137958331?text=...` |
| **United Kingdom (UK)** | `+44 7911 123456` | `447911123456` | `https://wa.me/447911123456?text=...` |
| **Pakistan** | `+92 370 3210078` | `923703210078` | `https://wa.me/923703210078?text=...` |

### Sanitization Rules:
1. Strip all non-digit characters (`+`, `-`, ` `, `(`, `)`).
2. If US/Canada number is 10 digits without leading 1 (e.g. `6137958331`), prepend `1` -> `16137958331`.
3. If UAE local number starts with `05` (e.g. `0581037571`), strip the leading `0` and prepend `971` -> `971581037571`.
4. Ignore toll-free numbers (e.g. `1-800`, `1-888`, `800`) and numbers with fewer than 7 digits — mark as `No Phone` or invalid.

## 4. WhatsApp Compliance & Anti-Ban Rules

To maintain high account reputation and prevent automated spam flags on WhatsApp Web:
- **Delay between sends:** Maintain a minimum 5–10 second delay between individual message dispatches.
- **Batching:** Send in controlled batches (e.g., 10–25 contacts per session).
- **Personalization Variance:** Every message must reference the unique business name, city, and observed audit issue. Never send identical duplicate copy to multiple numbers.
- **Immediate State Recording:** On every dispatch, record status immediately (`Sent`, `Not Found`, `No Phone`) in the CSV so contacts are never messaged twice.
