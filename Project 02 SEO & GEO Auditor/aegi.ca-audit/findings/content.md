# Content Quality & E-E-A-T Findings — aegi.ca

**Target Domain:** `https://www.aegi.ca/`  
**Analyzed Pages:** Homepage, Services (`/services/civil-engineering`), Blog (`/blog/*`), Team (`/team/*`)  
**Content Quality Score:** 84 / 100

---

## 1. Content Quality, E-E-A-T & Readability

### Finding 1.1: Strong Industry Credentials & Authentic Authorship Signals
- **Specific Observation (Evidence):**
  - High E-E-A-T signals are prominently featured:
    - Regulatory licenses: APEGA (Permit to Practice) and NAPEG permit holders prominently stated with permit validation.
    - Verified safety: Workers' Compensation Board (WCB) coverage badge.
    - Dedicated team member pages (`/team/adeel-abdullah`, `/team/khurshid-anwar`, etc.) highlighting licensed P.Eng professional engineers with biographies.
- **Action Step:**
  - Maintain and expand author bylines across all 13 blog posts. Ensure every article links to the specific P.Eng author's `/team/:slug` page with their licensing credentials to reinforce Google's Helpful Content and Quality Rater Guidelines.
- **Leading Indicator:**
  - Every blog article displays author name, P.Eng license designation, and internal link to their team bio.
- **Falsifiability Test:**
  - If any blog article has anonymous or generic "Admin" authorship, the E-E-A-T authorship attribution has failed.

---

### Finding 1.2: Boilerplate Repetition Across Service Modules
- **Specific Observation (Evidence):**
  - Content quality analysis flagged a 61% repetition index on the homepage due to duplicate micro-copy across 8 service cards and repeated value proposition phrases ("innovative projects", "deliverables", "novel construction solutions").
- **Concrete Action Step:**
  - Rewrite service descriptions to replace generic buzzwords with concrete, project-specific outcomes (e.g. turnaround time, municipal permit approvals in Edmonton/Calgary, exact software utilized such as Civil 3D or Revit).
- **Leading Indicator:**
  - Content repetition index drops below 35% when measured via automated text tokenization.
- **Falsifiability Test:**
  - If unique token ratio does not increase by at least 20% on re-audit, the content differentiation has failed.

---

### Finding 1.3: Blog-to-Service Internal Linking Gaps
- **Specific Observation (Evidence):**
  - AEGI has 13 high-value technical articles (e.g., `haul-road-design`, `oil-gas-pipeline-engineering-alberta`, `site-grading-plan-is-essential`, `stormwater-management-design`).
  - Many of these articles do not feature prominent contextual CTA text or in-content contextual links pointing visitors directly to the relevant service inquiry pages (`/services/civil-engineering`, `/services/machine-control-model`).
  - GSC search data shows queries like *"alberta civil engineering consultant"* hitting blog posts with 69 impressions and 0 clicks.
- **Concrete Action Step:**
  - Add contextual editorial internal links in the first 200 words and last paragraph of each blog post pointing directly to the relevant core service page with targeted anchor text (e.g. "civil engineering consulting in Alberta").
  - Add an inline consultation inquiry card at the bottom of every article.
- **Leading Indicator:**
  - Internal link count from blog posts to commercial service URLs increases by 100% (minimum 2 contextual links per article).
- **Falsifiability Test:**
  - If GSC click-through rate on blog URLs does not improve or assisted conversions remain zero after 45 days, the internal linking strategy has failed.
