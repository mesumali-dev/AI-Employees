## 2. LinkedIn (Company Page)
Should you call an engineer before starting a commercial renovation?

Yes. The cost of altering a design during the drafting stage is negligible. The cost of discovering a structural conflict, an undersized electrical panel, or non-compliant mechanical ducting during active construction can derail budgets and timelines by weeks.

When renovations involve removing walls, relocating washrooms, adding rooftop units, or modifying building envelopes, licensed engineering review is legally and structurally essential. Early engineering input ensures permit approvals move smoothly through local municipalities and trade contractors build with confidence.
