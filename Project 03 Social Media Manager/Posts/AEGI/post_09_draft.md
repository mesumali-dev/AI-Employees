## 2. LinkedIn (Company Page)
Accessibility should be designed into a project, not added later.

Retrofitting ramps, adjusting curb cuts, or reworking doorway grades after concrete has cured is astronomically more expensive than incorporating universal design principles during initial site planning.

Barrier-free accessibility encompasses far more than painting blue lines on parking stalls. It requires precise civil grading to ensure accessible stalls meet maximum cross-slope thresholds, continuous accessible pathways connect to municipal sidewalks, and entrance thresholds remain smooth and code-compliant under local building codes.

At Advanced Engineering Group Inc. (AEGI), our civil and structural engineers integrate barrier-free standards into site plans, parking layouts, and building entries from day one—fostering inclusive spaces that protect developers from compliance redesigns.
