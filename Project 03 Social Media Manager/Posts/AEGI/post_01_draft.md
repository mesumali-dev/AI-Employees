## 2. LinkedIn (Company Page)
Planning an expansion? Your existing building may need to evolve too.

Expanding an operational facility involves far more than simply constructing additional square footage. Introducing heavier equipment, extending floor space, adding mechanical units, or increasing occupancy immediately shifts structural load distributions, strains electrical supply, and alters ventilation and life-safety egress requirements.

When expansion planning occurs without comprehensive multidisciplinary coordination, unforeseen structural bottlenecks, electrical service overloads, and costly mid-construction redesigns inevitably arise. Early engineering coordination bridges the gap between architectural ambition and structural reality—evaluating structural integrity, mechanical capacity, and electrical distribution concurrently.

At Advanced Engineering Group Inc. (AEGI), our licensed P.Eng team across Alberta ensures facility expansion plans remain practical, compliant, and cost-effective from initial concept through completion.
