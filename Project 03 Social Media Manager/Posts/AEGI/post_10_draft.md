## 2. LinkedIn (Company Page)
A retaining wall holds back far more than just soil.

When designing earth retention systems, soil mass is only one component of the structural equation. Surcharge loads from adjacent parking traffic, foundation footings above the slope, seasonal freeze-thaw cycles, and trapped hydrostatic groundwater exert immense lateral pressures.

Without specialized geotechnical and structural engineering, inadequate drainage causes water to accumulate behind the wall, dramatically multiplying lateral loads until cracking, bowing, or catastrophic blowouts occur.

Engineered retaining walls protect site integrity. Advanced Engineering Group Inc. (AEGI) designs retaining systems that incorporate granular drainage backfill, perforated sub-drains, geogrid reinforcement, and structural stability calculations engineered for Alberta's demanding climate.
