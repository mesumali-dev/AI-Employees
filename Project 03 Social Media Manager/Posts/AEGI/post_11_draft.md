## 2. LinkedIn (Company Page)
Why can a few inches of grade make such a massive difference on a commercial development?

Good grading is often invisible. Its problems are not.

On paper, a 2% slope across a parking lot or landscaped buffer may look simple. In practice, a difference of just a few inches in elevation can invert drainage flow—funneling stormwater directly toward basement walls, pooling water at commercial entrances, or saturating subgrade soils under heavy truck lanes.

Comprehensive grading design synchronizes finished floor elevations, curb heights, swales, catch basins, and municipal tie-ins. When grading is engineered with precision, it protects property assets, prevents winter ice buildup, and reduces long-term pavement maintenance.
