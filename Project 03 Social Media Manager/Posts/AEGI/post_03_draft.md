## 2. LinkedIn (Company Page)
A warehouse built for today’s operations may struggle with tomorrow’s demands. Is your facility prepared for growth?

Modern logistics and warehousing move fast. Installing high-density racking, introducing automated material handling, and deploying electric forklift fleets introduces concentrated point loads and power demands that standard floor slabs and electrical services were never rated to support.

Upgrading operations without prior structural verification risks concrete slab cracking, differential settlement, and severe code violations. Comprehensive facility engineering evaluates slab load capacity, overhead truss clearance, MEP distribution, and equipment traffic flow before equipment arrives on site.
