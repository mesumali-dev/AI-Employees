## 2. LinkedIn (Company Page)
Is site drainage really just about moving rainwater?

No. Drainage impacts far more than surface water flow. Improperly graded sites and inadequate stormwater conveyance can saturate subsoils, undermine building foundations, accelerate asphalt deterioration, and create hazardous standing water in parking lots.

Effective civil site engineering plans for extreme weather events, soil percolation rates, site topography, and municipal stormwater regulations. By engineering custom catchments, retention systems, and sub-surface drainage, developers protect their capital investments from long-term erosion and settlement damage.

Good site design manages water before water manages the site. Advanced Engineering Group Inc. (AEGI) delivers licensed civil engineering, site grading, and stormwater management solutions throughout Alberta.
