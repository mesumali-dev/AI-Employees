Is an older building still suitable for today's commercial requirements?

Not always. Buildings experience continuous wear and functional shifts over their lifespan. Changing tenant types, modern mechanical equipment, heavy IT infrastructure, and updated provincial building codes place demands on older structures that they were never originally engineered to handle.

Before purchasing, renovating, or repurposing an existing asset, an engineering condition assessment is critical.
